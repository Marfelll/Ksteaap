Kstep — every step closer to your next job.

Short description
A job search app built around your portfolio, not just your résumé. Show your work, get matched with companies looking for it.

Full description
Kstep helps you find work by showing what you can actually do. Build a profile that leads with your portfolio, not a wall of text. Add the projects you're proud of, and let hiring teams see your work before they see your résumé.

Kstep matches you with companies based on your skills and the work in your portfolio, not keywords in a job title. Track how your profile is performing, see who's viewed it, and follow every application in one place.

Three things, done well: a profile that shows who you are, a portfolio that shows what you can do, and a list of companies worth applying to.

Structure (three sections)

Profile — your info, skills, and activity, in one place
Portfolio — the work that gets you hired
Companies — matches based on your skills, not your keywords
